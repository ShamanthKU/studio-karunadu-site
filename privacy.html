Studio Karunadu

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Who we are

Studio Karunadu is an independent creator studio that publishes documentary and short-form videos. Our publishing tools (the "Studio Karunadu Uploader" and "Studio Karunadu Publisher" apps) are used only by Studio Karunadu itself, to manage our own channel and accounts.

What the apps access

  • Google / YouTube: with the channel owner's permission, the app uploads and schedules our own videos, edits their titles and descriptions, and reads analytics for our own channel (permissions: YouTube upload, YouTube manage, YouTube Analytics read-only).
  • Meta (Instagram / Facebook): with the account owner's permission, the app publishes our own posts and Reels to our own Instagram professional account and Facebook Page and reads their basic insights.

What we do with the data

The apps handle only the data of the studio's own accounts. We do not collect, sell, share or transfer personal data of any other person. Access tokens are stored only in private, access-restricted storage used by our automation and are never published. Analytics are used only to plan our own content.

Studio Karunadu's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Tokens and cached analytics are kept only while the automation is in use. You can revoke access at any time: for Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and for Meta in your Facebook/Instagram settings under "Apps and websites". To request deletion of any data we hold, email studiokarunadu@gmail.com.
